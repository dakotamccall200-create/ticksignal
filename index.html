How it works

1
You photograph it. One phone photo, three taps — photo, location, done. EXIF data is stripped on intake and your location is coarsened to county before anything is stored.
2
The AI suggests a species. A top-3 guess with confidence in seconds. It's a suggestion, not a diagnosis — never medical advice.
3
A human expert confirms or corrects. Volunteer entomologists review the queue. Their corrections become training data.
4
The map updates, the AI retrains. Confirmed sightings land on the public county map. Every Friday the model retrains and the dashboard publishes the new accuracy — up or down.

🎯 Why we publish our misses

Most AI projects only advertise wins. TickSignal publishes the full confusion matrix — every species the AI confused for another — so the public can watch the model actually learn. Watch the AI learn →

Honest status

TickSignal is in pilot: the site is live, but there is no live AI classifier yet and no submission backend — the demo result and local-only storage are labeled where they appear. No money is being collected yet. Every figure is labeled SAMPLE, BASELINE, or DEMO so you always know what you're looking at. See the public roadmap for what launch still needs.
