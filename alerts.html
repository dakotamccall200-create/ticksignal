Free county sighting alerts

One email a week — only in weeks your county has new expert-confirmed tick or mosquito sightings. No spam, no empty "nothing happened" emails.

What you'll get

  • Sighting counts only: "4 new confirmed sightings in Barry County this week," with a species breakdown and a link to the public map.
  • Never medical advice. These emails do not assess your personal risk, diagnose anything, or recommend treatment. Bitten? See a clinician — CDC · Michigan DHHS.
  • County-level only. We never collect or share addresses, GPS, or names. Your email is used for these alerts and nothing else.
  • Free while in pilot. Unsubscribe anytime with one click — every email carries the link.

Sign up

How it works

  1. You pick a county. We store your email + county — nothing else.
  2. Each week we count newly confirmed sightings per county.
  3. If your county has new ones, you get one email. If not, silence.

PILOT honesty: the alert system is new and the sighting database is still growing, so early weeks may be quiet. That's real data, not a bug.

TickSignal is a public citizen-science pilot by Dakota McCall, Hastings, MI. Not medical advice.
