Why misses are published

  • Trust: an AI that admits mistakes is one you can calibrate.
  • Science: the misses are exactly what the model retrains on.
  • Safety: knowing the AI confuses lone star with dog tick 8% of the time matters more than knowing it gets blacklegged right 89% of the time.
