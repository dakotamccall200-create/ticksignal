About TickSignal

The mission

Tick-borne disease is exploding in Michigan — Lyme cases went from 553 in 2022 to 2,167 in 2025 (MDHHS). The public already photographs ticks by the thousand; an epidemiologist at MDHHS received over 1,000 tick-sighting photos in 2026 alone.

TickSignal turns those photos into public science: AI species ID in seconds, human expert confirmation, a live county risk map, and a public dashboard that publishes the AI's real accuracy every week — so anyone can watch the model learn. Nobody else does all four in one free, public project.

The method (the loop)

1
Submit — a phone photo, coarsened to county, EXIF stripped.
2
Suggest — the AI returns a top-3 species guess with confidence. A suggestion, never a diagnosis.
3
Confirm — volunteer entomologists and public-health partners confirm or correct.
4
Publish — the dot lands on the map; the photo joins the training set; Friday's retrain updates the public accuracy chart.

Binding rules: species ID + public info only, never medical advice · coarse location only, no names on the map · honest accuracy, misses published · expert gating before anything is treated as confirmed · nothing published or solicited until the founder approves.

The people

Dakota McCall — Founder, Hastings, Michigan. A dad of two young kids who play outside, building the tool he wishes existed when he found a tick and couldn't get a straight answer about what it was. Michigan is the current front line of Lyme expansion — so the project starts at home.

Muse — AI research agent. Designs, builds, and operates the pipeline: intake, ID model, validation routing, map and dashboard publishing, weekly retraining. All work is done on free tiers and open data — $0 spent.

Expert reviewers — recruiting. Volunteer entomologists (tick taxonomy), a university Extension liaison, and county public-health partners. Listed with credentials on the dashboard once they agree.

Contact

Health departments, Extension offices, and entomologists interested in the validation lane: the contact channel opens with launch. Until then, this demo is local-only and collects nothing.
