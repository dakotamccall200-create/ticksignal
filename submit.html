📸 Submit a photo

Three steps: photo → location → review. Photos are EXIF-stripped in your browser before upload; no name or contact info is collected.

1 · The photo

Tip: get close, steady, and fill the frame. Your photo is downscaled to max 1600 px and re-encoded as JPEG in your browser — this strips ALL EXIF location and metadata before anything is uploaded.

2 · Location COARSE ONLY

We only ever store your county. Pick it, or let us coarsen your GPS:

If you tap GPS, coordinates are rounded to ~0.1° (≈11 km) in your browser, matched to the nearest county, and the precise point is never stored — only the county name goes with your submission.

County-level only — don't include street addresses or precise locations.

3 · Review & submit

Every submission is reviewed by a volunteer entomologist before it appears on the public map. Our research identification model runs over new photos in the weekly pipeline — results feed the public accuracy dashboard.
